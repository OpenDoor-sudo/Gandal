# Student Profile: Economics

## Where They Excel
* Understanding the fundamental link between health and economic development.
* Identifying concrete, direct economic impacts (e.g., reduced production, lack of school attendance) once questions are simplified.
* Identifying key characteristics of a right triangle (sides, angles, longest side)
* Applying the Pythagorean theorem to calculate unknown side lengths
* Understanding the inverse application of the Pythagorean theorem to find other sides
* Grasping the connection between public health measures (like access to potable water and vaccination) and economic productivity.
* Summarizing complex ideas accurately after receiving explanations and examples.
* Applying associativity property for addition (e.g., 7+4+3)
* Correctly performing initial steps in multi-number addition (e.g., 9+15=24)
* Recognizing and articulating progress in mental calculation

## Where They Struggle
* Understanding abstract or complex questions without prior simplification or concrete examples.
* Broadening perspective to identify indirect impacts without explicit prompting or examples.
* Recalling the precise measurement of the right angle (initially 98°, then 80° instead of 90°)
* Precise terminology (e.g., confusing 'triangle carré' with 'triangle rectangle', or 'hypoténuse' in plural)
* Recalling specific solutions or details from video content without prompting.
* Independently elaborating on the diverse economic impacts of improved health without concrete examples.
* Accuracy in adding two-digit numbers when not broken down (e.g., initially calculated 24+12 as 46)
* Requires prompting to decompose multi-digit addition into units and tens
