👥 Collaborative Pod Controls

You and your desk partners share synchronized video playback. Pressing Play, Pause, or Seek updates all screens in your pod in real time.

🎙️ Voice Priority Interlock

Tap the Click to Talk mic button to ask a question. The video will automatically pause across all pod screens and lock other microphones so nobody talks over each other.

At the end of each lesson, Tab 2 unlocks for your evaluation. Every member of the pod must individually achieve ≥85% to unlock the next chapter playlist together.
